What does this score mean?

The TalentLens compatibility score estimates how closely your resume aligns with the selected job description. It considers relevant skills, keywords, experience, projects, and other job-specific information identified during the analysis.

80–100 — Strong Match: Your resume aligns well with the job requirements.
60–79 — Moderate Match: Your resume has relevant experience, but some important requirements may be missing.
40–59 — Needs Improvement: Several important skills or keywords may be missing.
Below 40 — Low Match: Your resume has limited alignment with the current job description.
